生成式 AI 應用於護理交班紀錄之可行性評估：以某區域教學醫院為例 Feasibility Assessment of Generative AI Applied to Nursing Handoff Documentation: A Case Study of a Regional Teaching Hospital

指導教授：李偉柏 博士Advisor: Dr. Wei-Po Lee

護理師每班都要花大量時間在多個系統間查資料、手寫交班紀錄。我建構了一套 RAG + 大型語言模型 的自動產生流程，整合 17 類臨床資料、學習護理師的書寫風格，自動產出交班紀錄草稿，讓護理師從「書寫者」轉為「審閱者」。 Nurses spend a large share of every shift digging through multiple systems to write handoff notes. I built a RAG + LLM pipeline that pulls 17 types of clinical data, learns the nurses' own writing style and drafts the handoff note — turning nurses from writers into reviewers.

關鍵發現Key findings
發現並解決「跨班雪球效應」Found and fixed the cross-shift snowball effect

每班輸出會成為下一班的輸入，內容會越滾越長。我設計五步驟的前班輸出壓縮機制，讓長度維持穩定。Each shift's output feeds the next, so notes balloon over time. A five-step prior-shift compression keeps length stable.

資料層過濾比 Prompt 禁令有效Filtering data beats prompt rules

模型看不到的內容就不會寫。Parser、Context、Prompt、Output 四層一起過濾雜訊，ROUGE-L 提升 18–22%。What the model never sees, it never writes. Four filtering layers (parser, context, prompt, output) lifted ROUGE-L by 18–22%.

安全審查清單讓幻覺減少 34.7%A safety checklist cut hallucinations by 34.7%

在 Prompt 中加入管路、過敏、DNR、用藥劑量等六項檢查，病人安全分數提升 11%。Six prompt-level checks (lines, allergies, DNR, dosage…) raised the safety score by 11%.

以成本效益做模型選型Model choice driven by cost and quality

比較 gemma、gpt-oss、gpt-5-mini 等模型。gpt-5-mini 品質已達高原，成本只有 gpt-4o 的 1/8.4。Compared gemma, gpt-oss and gpt-5-mini; gpt-5-mini hit the quality plateau at 1/8.4 the cost of gpt-4o.

延伸應用Beyond healthcare「從多個系統彙整資料、參考歷史紀錄、產生草稿再由專人確認」這套做法，同樣適用於產線交接班日誌、設備維修紀錄等需要跨班延續的現場文件。The same pattern — gather data from several systems, draw on past records, draft, then have a person confirm — fits shift-handover logs and equipment maintenance records on the factory floor just as well.
